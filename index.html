Frequently Asked Questions

What type of buildings can you make using PUF panels? expand_more
We build warehouses, factories, showrooms, shops, offices, rooms, farm houses, site offices, and prefab buildings using high-quality PUF panels.
Do you manufacture PUF panels? expand_more
No, we do not manufacture panels. Our business focuses on designing and constructing buildings using PUF panels with professional installation.
How long does it take to complete a PUF panel building? expand_more
Construction is much faster than traditional buildings. Most projects like warehouses or offices can be completed within a few weeks depending on size.
Are PUF panel buildings strong and durable? expand_more
Yes. PUF panel structures are strong, weather resistant, and long lasting. They provide good insulation and perform well in both summer and winter conditions.
Can you build custom size buildings? expand_more
Yes, we design buildings based on your requirements. Whether you need a small shop or a large warehouse, we can customize the size and layout.
Is a PUF panel building energy efficient? expand_more
Yes. PUF panels provide excellent insulation which helps keep the building cool in summer and reduces electricity usage.
Do you provide complete building construction? expand_more
Yes, we provide complete solutions including structure design, PUF panel installation, roofing, and finishing work.
Are PUF panel buildings suitable for offices and showrooms? expand_more
Yes, many businesses use PUF panel buildings for offices, showrooms, and shops because they look modern and can be built quickly.
Do you work across India? expand_more
Yes, we take projects in different cities depending on project size and requirements.
How can I get a quotation for my project? expand_more
You can contact us by phone or WhatsApp and share your project details like location, size, and type of building. Our team will provide a quotation.
